ELECTROACOUSTICS

REFERENCE SHEET

Chapter 1 – Levels of Electroacoustic Quantities

This reference sheet provides essential formulas, concepts, and relationships for Chapter 1 of Electroacoustics. Each section is organized for clarity and quick access during study and problem-solving.

Section Concept Formula/Rule Explanation/Use Case Example/Notes
Fundamental Definitions
(Pale Blue) 		Bel Definition
(1.1) A(Bel) = log₁₀(P₂ / P₁)
Definition of bel: ratio of two powers 1 Bel = 10 dB
Decibel Definition
(1.2) A(dB) = 10 log₁₀(P₂ / P₁)
Definition of decibel: 10 times the logarithmic power ratio 10:1 power ratio = 10 dB
Amplitude/Voltage Levels
(1.3) L = 20 log₁₀(a₂ / a₁)
For voltage, pressure, or amplitude quantities 2:1 voltage ratio = 6.02 dB
Electrical References
(Pale Green) 		dBm Reference
(1.4) L(dBm) = 10 log₁₀(P / 1 mW)
Electrical power level relative to 1 milliwatt Reference: 1 mW
dBu Reference
(1.5) L(dBu) = 20 log₁₀(V / 0.775 V)
Voltage level relative to 0.775 V RMS (≈ 1 mW into 600Ω) Reference: 0.775 V RMS
Acoustic Measurements
(Pale Pink) 		Sound Pressure Level
(1.6) L_SPL = 20 log₁₀(p / 20 µPa)
Sound pressure level (SPL), reference = 20 µPa (threshold of hearing) Reference: 20 µPa
RMS Pressure
(1.7) p_RMS = √[(1/T) ∫₀ᵀ p²(t) dt]
RMS value of acoustic pressure (for periodic/stationary signals) Time-averaged pressure
RMS Voltage
(1.8) V_RMS = √[(1/T) ∫₀ᵀ V²(t) dt]
RMS value of electrical voltage (for periodic/stationary signals) Effective voltage value
Average Power
(1.9) W_avg = (1/T) ∫₀ᵀ W(t) dt
Average (RMS) power over time (for periodic/stationary signals) Time-averaged power
Multiple Sources & SNR
(Pale Teal) 		Pressure Superposition
(1.10) p(t) = p₁(t) + p₂(t) + p₃(t) + ...
Instantaneous pressure superposition from independent sources Linear addition
Mean-Square Pressure
(1.11) p_total² = p₁² + p₂² + p₃² + ...
Total mean square pressure for uncorrelated sources Power addition
Total Level Combination
(1.12) L_total = 10 log₁₀(10^(L₁/10) + 10^(L₂/10) + ...)
Total level from multiple independent sources Two equal 60 dB sources = 63.01 dB
Background + Source
(1.13) L_total = 10 log₁₀(10^(L₀/10) + 10^(L₁/10))
Combined background noise + source noise Noise floor analysis
Source Isolation
(1.14) L₁ = 10 log₁₀(10^(L_total/10) - 10^(L₀/10))
Isolating one source from background noise Signal extraction
Propagation & Ratios
(Pale Orange) 		SNR (Pressure)
(1.15) SNR = 20 log₁₀(p₁/p₂)
Signal-to-noise ratio (acoustic pressures, same conditions) Acoustic SNR
SNR (Voltage)
(1.16) SNR = 20 log₁₀(V₁/V₂)
Signal-to-noise ratio (voltages, same conditions) Electrical SNR
SNR (Level Difference)
(1.17) SNR = L₁ - L₂
Difference of levels (signal minus noise) 80 dB - 60 dB = 20 dB SNR
Free-Field Propagation
(1.18) L₂ - L₁ = -20 log₁₀(r₂/r₁)
Free-field propagation law (pressure drops with distance) 6 dB drop per distance doubling
Pressure-Power Relation
(1.19) 20 log₁₀(p₂/p₁) = 10 log₁₀(W₂/W₁)
Fundamental relationship between pressure and power ratios (since power ∝ pressure²) 2:1 pressure = 4:1 power

📝 Key Reference Values

Common Ratios: 2:1 voltage = +6.02 dB | 10:1 voltage = +20 dB | 2:1 power = +3.01 dB | 10:1 power = +10 dB

Standard References: SPL = 20 µPa | dBu = 0.775 V RMS | dBm = 1 mW
